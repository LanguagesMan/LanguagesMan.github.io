Languy

There is exactly one superpower a human can actually get. And it's learnable.

Speaking someone's own language changes what they'll say to you, what opens, and who you get to be anywhere on earth. It isn't talent and it isn't school — it's a method. I used it to get to 15.

The method, in the order it works

  1. Ears before mouth. You understand long before you speak. Stop forcing output.
  2. One frame at a time. Learn a sentence you can bend, not a word you can only recite.
  3. Say it out loud, immediately. Once. Badly. That's the rep.
  4. Input you actually like. Boring material is the reason people quit, not difficulty.
  5. Mistakes are the scoring system. The careful learner loses. Every time.
  6. Real people, early. Sooner than you feel ready — that's the whole point.
  7. Show up small, daily. Ten honest minutes beats a heroic Sunday.
