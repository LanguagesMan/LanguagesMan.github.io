Stuff people ask me

What even is this?
A way of learning built on understanding real input instead of grammar drills — and the material to do it with. The method is free to start. If you want me to teach it to you, that's $9 for the first chapter and $97 for all nine — played, not watched. If you just want the practice material, that's $39 for one language.
How much time a day?
Fifteen honest minutes beats two distracted hours. Most of it can ride on things you already do — the show you were going to watch anyway, the commute you were going to spend scrolling.
Is there an app?
Not a phone app. Everything runs in your browser, on any device, and the files are yours to keep. Nothing here can be taken away from you by an update.
What if it's not for me?
30-day money-back on everything, no questions. One reply to me and you're refunded. I'd rather have that than someone sitting on something they don't use.
Which languages?
Spanish, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese have full kits today — 1,307 to 1,393 sentences each, all with audio. The method itself works for any language; the ready-made fuel is those seven so far. See what's inside a kit.
Will this work for me?
It works if you actually put the input in. It doesn't work as something you buy and feel good about owning. That's the honest answer — take the free door first and see whether the thing above (one word changing at a time) does anything for you.
