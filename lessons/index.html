The method, in the order it works

  1. Ears before mouth. You understand long before you can speak. Stop forcing output.
  2. One frame at a time. Learn a sentence you can bend — not a word you can only recite.
  3. Say it out loud, immediately. Once. Badly. That's the rep.
  4. Input you actually like. Boring material is why people quit — not difficulty.
  5. Mistakes are the scoring system. The careful learner loses. Every time.
  6. Real people, early. Sooner than you feel ready. That's the whole point.
  7. Show up small, daily. Ten honest minutes beats a heroic Sunday.
