Privacy & Refunds

Privacy, in plain language

If you join the email list, we store your name and email with Brevo (our email provider) and use them for one thing: sending you the emails you signed up for. Every email has an unsubscribe link that works instantly. We don't sell, rent, or share your data with anyone, ever.

Purchases are processed by Stripe — they handle your payment data and we never see your card number. Stripe's privacy policy applies at checkout.

This site itself sets no tracking cookies. To remove your data from our list, click unsubscribe or email hello@languyofficial.com and it's done within 7 days.

Refunds (the 30-Day Dropout Insurance)

Any digital product here: full refund within 30 days of purchase, no questions, no forms — reply to your receipt email or write to hello@languyofficial.com. Languy Daily subscriptions can be cancelled anytime and stop billing immediately at the next cycle.
